Independent Chrome extension

BenefitsCal Login Helper

Fewer manual steps when you sign in.

Chrome autofills your saved BenefitsCal credentials. The helper activates the login button, finds the new email verification code in your connected Gmail or Outlook mailbox, and enters and submits that code.

How the helper works

  1. Connect the mailbox that receives your BenefitsCal verification emails using Google or Microsoft OAuth.
  2. Enable automatic sign-in and open BenefitsCal in Chrome with your saved credentials.
  3. The helper checks for a fresh verification email during the login attempt and submits its code.

The helper runs in your browser. It does not use a developer-operated server to process mail. Gmail access uses the Gmail API and the read-only gmail.readonly permission; Outlook access uses Microsoft Graph's delegated Mail.Read permission.

These permissions allow reading your mailbox. The helper limits its requests to recent messages from no-reply@login.benefitscal.org with the subject Your BenefitsCal Verification Code. Google does not offer a permission limited to this sender.

Privacy policy

Effective November 4, 2026.

Data accessed and purpose

With your authorization, the extension reads matching email headers, timestamps, and message bodies solely to obtain the verification code for your current BenefitsCal sign-in. The code is entered on the BenefitsCal verification page. The extension checks whether the login fields are filled and activates the site's login controls; it does not copy your password into extension storage or messages.

Storage and retention

Mailbox content and verification codes are processed temporarily in memory and are not deliberately saved or logged by the extension. Provider choice, OAuth client configuration, and the automation preference are stored locally in Chrome. Login-attempt metadata and mailbox connection status are stored in Chrome session storage. Outlook access and refresh tokens are held in session storage. Google tokens are managed by Chrome Identity and its token cache. Restarting Chrome or reloading the extension requires reconnecting the helper.

Sharing and use limitations

The extension communicates directly with Google or Microsoft for authorization and mail retrieval, and with BenefitsCal through its sign-in page. The developer does not receive your email, password, tokens, or verification codes through the extension. The extension contains no advertising or analytics and does not sell data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. BenefitsCal Login Helper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and deletion

Use Disconnect & pause in the extension to stop automation and clear its connection state and cached tokens. This does not revoke the provider's authorization grant. To revoke access, remove the helper in your Google Account's third-party connections or your Microsoft account's app permissions. Uninstalling the extension removes its extension storage. The developer has no server-side mailbox records to delete.

This website and support

This informational website uses no analytics scripts, forms, or cookies of its own. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical request information under GitHub's privacy statement. If you email support, the maintainer receives the information you choose to send and uses it to respond. Do not send passwords, access tokens, verification codes, or benefits case details.

Terms of use

Use this helper only with accounts you own or are authorized to access. You remain responsible for your account and for reviewing activity on the BenefitsCal website.

This personal utility is provided as-is, without a guarantee of availability or successful login. Website changes, mailbox permissions, or browser behavior can interrupt automation. You can always pause the extension and sign in manually.

The helper only assists with sign-in. It does not submit benefits applications, report changes, determine eligibility, or provide benefits advice. Automating email verification reduces the separation between your password and email verification on this browser; use a trusted device and protect access to your Chrome profile.
